Price movement

Delta estimates how option value responds to movement in the underlying. Gamma explains how that Delta itself changes as price moves.

Time

Theta shows the effect of time decay. Understanding its curve is essential when comparing short- and long-premium positions.

Volatility

Vega measures sensitivity to implied volatility, helping explain why option prices can change even when the underlying barely moves.
