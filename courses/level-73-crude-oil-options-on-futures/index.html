Mastering Crude Oil Options on Futures

Crude oil prices are influenced by geopolitical events, supply and demand dynamics, OPEC policies and macroeconomic trends. This advanced course focuses on interpreting these market drivers and using futures options to hedge risk or express a market view.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand the main drivers of crude oil price volatility.
  • Learn options contract specifications, pricing, margin and expiration cycles.
  • Apply vertical spreads, straddles and strangles.
  • Use crude oil options to hedge price risk and portfolio exposure.
  • Study strategy responses to supply disruptions and price shocks.

Why This Course Is Important

Crude oil options require careful analysis of both market direction and volatility. The renewed course will provide a practical framework for managing risk and evaluating trading opportunities in changing energy-market conditions.
