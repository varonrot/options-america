Optimize the Portfolio, Not Just the Trade

This course moves beyond individual positions. It shows how multiple bullish, bearish and neutral strategies interact, and how beta-weighting helps translate them into a common measure of market exposure.

Start This Course →

Build and Measure Portfolio Exposure

Start with market assumptions, portfolio construction and risk principles. Then use beta-weighted Delta to evaluate positions against the S&P 500 and understand how the portfolio may respond to a market move.

  • Understand beta-weighting and benchmark selection.
  • Measure bullish, bearish and neutral exposure.
  • Choose liquid options and defined-risk structures.
  • Diversify across strategies and market assumptions.

Manage Delta, Theta and Vega Together

Apply practical adjustment techniques to manage direction, time decay and volatility exposure across multiple positions, supported by calculations, case studies and real-world portfolio scenarios.

  • Hedge and rebalance Delta as markets move.
  • Build positive Theta while controlling directional risk.
  • Adjust Vega exposure when volatility changes.
  • Use case studies to connect the Greeks to portfolio decisions.
