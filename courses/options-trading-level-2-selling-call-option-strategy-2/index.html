Learn the Basics of Selling Call Options

This course continues the Options America beginner path with the seller's perspective. It focuses on how a short call is structured, how premium income works, and why the risk profile differs significantly from buying a call.

Understand Risk, Reward and Trade Structure

You will learn how strike selection, expiration and the underlying price influence the position, and why managing risk is essential when selling options.

  • Understand the rights and obligations of the call seller.
  • Learn how premium is received and when it may be retained.
  • Compare selling calls with buying calls.
  • Build a foundation for covered calls and advanced short-option strategies.
