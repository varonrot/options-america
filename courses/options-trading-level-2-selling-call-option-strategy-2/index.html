Learn Selling Call Options from the Seller's Perspective

This course explains the structure of a short call position and the relationship between the call buyer and seller. You will learn why the seller receives a premium, what obligation is created, and how the seller's market assumption differs from the buyer's.

Level 2 builds directly on the call-option concepts introduced in Level 1 and shifts the focus from paying a premium for a right to receiving a premium while accepting an obligation.

Analyze Profit, Loss and Option Chains

The lessons move from core definitions into practical profit-and-loss analysis, option-chain selection, in-the-money and out-of-the-money contracts, expiration choices and seller-focused scenarios.

You will see how the same underlying stock move can produce very different outcomes for a call buyer and a call seller, and why strike selection and time to expiration matter when evaluating the trade.

  • Understand the rights of the buyer and obligations of the seller.
  • Compare buyer and seller profit-and-loss profiles.
  • Analyze option chains from the call seller's perspective.
  • Work through Apple price scenarios and T+0 behavior.
  • Build the foundation for covered calls and advanced short-option strategies.

Understand Premium, Expiration and Seller Risk

Selling an option is not simply the opposite button from buying one. The course explains how premium income interacts with the seller's obligation and why risk must be understood before entering a short-option position.

  • Identify the premium received when opening a short call.
  • Understand how expiration affects the position.
  • Connect stock-price movement to the seller's profit-and-loss graph.
  • Recognize why risk management is essential when selling options.

Work Through Real Trading Scenarios

The final sections connect the theory to market assumptions, strike selection and changing stock prices. The course finishes with T+0 scenarios and a transition into practical selling-call demonstrations.

Course requirements

  • Level 1 or equivalent understanding of buying call options is recommended.
  • Basic familiarity with strike price, expiration and option premium.
  • No paid membership is required.

Intended audience

  • Beginner options traders continuing from Level 1.
  • Traders who want to understand the option seller's perspective.
  • Investors preparing to learn covered calls and income strategies.
