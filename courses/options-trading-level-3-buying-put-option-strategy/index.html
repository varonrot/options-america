Learn the Basics of Buying Put Options

This course introduces the mechanics of long puts and shows how puts can be used for bearish market views or downside protection.

Understand Risk and Reward

You will examine premium, strike price, expiration and breakeven, and learn how those inputs shape the payoff profile of a long put position.

  • Understand the right held by a put buyer.
  • Learn how put value responds to changes in the underlying asset.
  • Compare long puts with long calls.
  • Build a foundation for protective puts and bearish spread strategies.
