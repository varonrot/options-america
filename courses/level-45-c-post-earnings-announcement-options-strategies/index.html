Trade After the Earnings Reaction

Once the report is released, implied volatility, price direction and market expectations reset quickly. This course shows how to interpret that reaction and manage or initiate positions with a structured post-earnings plan.

Start This Course →

Analyze Direction and Volatility

Use IV Rank, IV Percentile, price action and the continuation or reversal of the earnings move to identify the most suitable options structure.

  • Understand the post-earnings volatility reset.
  • Analyze directional continuation after the report.
  • Interpret IV Rank and IV Percentile after earnings.
  • Match the strategy to the updated market outlook.

Manage Positions Through Practical Case Studies

Study straddles, call spreads, short strangles, short puts and diagonal spreads using real stock examples, then apply portfolio and risk-management principles.

  • Manage straddles after the announcement.
  • Review bullish, bearish and neutral case studies.
  • Monitor Greeks and changing volatility.
  • Diversify, use stop-loss rules and adjust positions.
