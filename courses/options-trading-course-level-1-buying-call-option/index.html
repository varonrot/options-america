Buying Call Options: Start Here

A long call is one of the simplest ways to gain bullish exposure using options. The buyer pays a premium for the right, but not the obligation, to buy the underlying asset at a specified strike price before expiration.

This course walks through the mechanics step by step. You will learn what changes the value of a call option, how to choose a strike and expiration, how to calculate breakeven, and why the premium paid represents the maximum possible loss.

📈Payoff Diagram / Course VisualOptional image path ready for later

What you will learn

  • How a call option contract works and what rights the buyer receives.
  • Why buying a call is generally considered a bullish strategy.
  • How strike price, expiration and premium affect the position.
  • How to calculate maximum loss, breakeven and potential upside.
  • How time decay and implied volatility can affect the option before expiration.

Course requirements

  • No prior options trading experience is required.
  • Basic understanding of stocks and market prices is helpful.
  • A willingness to learn risk before focusing on potential returns.

Intended audience

  • Beginners starting their options education.
  • Stock investors who want to understand bullish option alternatives.
  • Traders who want a structured foundation before moving to advanced strategies.
