Course Overview

This course introduces the long call strategy and the core logic behind buying call options. It is designed as the first step in the Options America learning path, before moving into short calls, puts, Greeks and multi-leg strategies.

What you will learn

✓ What a call option gives the buyer the right to do.
✓ How strike price and expiration affect the trade.
✓ Why a long call is generally a bullish strategy.
✓ How maximum loss is limited to the premium paid.
✓ How breakeven is calculated at expiration.
✓ How to think about time decay and volatility risk.

Who this course is for

New options traders who want a structured introduction to call options before progressing to more advanced strategies. No previous options trading experience is required.

How the course is structured

The course is broken into short lessons that move from basic concepts to practical trade analysis. Each lesson will eventually have its own dedicated page, so students can move through the material in sequence.
