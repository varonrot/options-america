Learn the Basics of Buying Call Options

This course introduces you to the essentials of buying call options, a fundamental strategy in options trading. Designed for beginners, it explains the core concepts of call options, including strike prices, expiration dates, premiums and the factors that influence an option's value.

You will learn what a call option gives the buyer the right to do, why traders use calls when they expect a market to rise, and how the premium paid defines the position's maximum loss.

Learn How to Analyze Market Trends

You will learn how to analyze market trends to identify situations where buying a call option may fit a bullish view. The course explains how to think about the relationship between the underlying stock price, the selected strike price and the expiration date.

Rather than focusing only on whether a stock may rise, you will learn why the timing and size of that move matter when choosing an option contract.

Build Key Risk Management Skills

Risk management is part of the strategy from the beginning. You will evaluate potential risk and reward, understand the premium at risk, and learn why position sizing and predefined exit decisions are important when trading options.

  • Understand that the premium paid can be lost if the option expires without value.
  • Compare potential upside with the amount of capital placed at risk.
  • Think about exits and trade management before entering the position.

Learn to Use Trading Platforms

The course also prepares you to work with an options trading platform. You will learn the practical information to look for when reviewing a call option contract, including the underlying price, strike, expiration and option premium.

The goal is to make the transition from theory to practical trade analysis easier when you begin reviewing real option chains.

Learn Through Real-World Case Studies

Real-world examples are used to show how buying call strategies behave under different market outcomes. These examples help connect the concepts of strike selection, expiration, premium, breakeven and price movement to actual trading decisions.

Benefit from a Comprehensive Learning Experience

The course combines structured explanations, examples and practical exercises so you can build a strong foundation in options trading. The objective is not simply to memorize terminology, but to understand how the pieces of a long-call trade work together.

By the end of the course, you should be ready to evaluate a basic buying-call setup and continue to the next courses in the Options America learning path.

Course requirements

  • No prior options trading experience is required.
  • A basic understanding of stocks and market prices is helpful.
  • You should be willing to learn risk and trade structure before focusing on returns.

Intended audience

  • Beginners who want to learn options trading from the ground up.
  • Stock investors who want to understand how call options can create bullish exposure.
  • Traders who want a structured foundation before moving to Greeks and multi-leg strategies.
