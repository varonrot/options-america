Understand Theta

This course focuses on theta, the option Greek associated with the effect of time on option value. You will learn why option value can decline even when the underlying asset does not move.

See Time Decay in Practice

You will examine how expiration changes the behavior of option positions and why time can work differently for buyers and sellers.

  • Understand what theta measures.
  • Learn why time decay accelerates near expiration.
  • Compare the effect of theta on long and short options.
  • Build a foundation for multi-leg income strategies.
