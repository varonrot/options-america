Learn how market leadership shifts through the economic cycle and how options can express sector views while controlling position, volatility and correlation risk.

Follow Changes in Market Leadership

Sector rotation connects the business cycle with the changing performance of technology, financials, energy, healthcare, consumer and other market groups. The course builds a practical framework for recognizing those shifts.

Translate Sector Views into Options Trades

Compare sector ETFs and individual stocks, evaluate liquidity and implied volatility, then choose calls, puts, vertical spreads, covered calls or collars to match the market view.

  • Identify early-, mid- and late-cycle leadership.
  • Measure relative strength and sector momentum.
  • Select liquid sector instruments and option structures.
  • Choose strikes, expirations and position size.

Manage Risk Across a Rotating Portfolio

The curriculum covers correlation, concentration, broad-market hedging, trade adjustments and exit rules, followed by sector case studies, simulated rotations and backtesting.

  • Control correlation and concentration risk.
  • React when sector leadership changes.
  • Practice across five major market sectors.
  • Build a repeatable long-term rotation process.
