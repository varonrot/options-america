Check the changing Greeks

Delta, gamma, theta and vega are snapshots around current inputs. In vix vs realized volatility, the dominant Greek can change as price moves or expiration approaches. Recalculate after a meaningful move and review the portfolio total; offsetting today's delta does not neutralize tomorrow's gamma or volatility exposure.

Plan execution and liquidity

Execution belongs in the analysis, not in a footnote. Estimate entry and exit slippage for vix vs realized volatility, check whether each leg trades actively and understand what happens at expiration. A strategy with a small theoretical edge may have no practical edge after two trips through a wide market.

Define management before entry

Management is a sequence of new choices, not a way to erase history. Preserve the original cost and every subsequent debit or credit in vix vs realized volatility. Recalculate the remaining payoff after any adjustment and compare it with the alternative of closing and holding no position.
